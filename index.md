# Privacy Policy — Massar

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**

---

## Overview
Massar collects user data to operate its mobile learning platform. The policy outlines what information is gathered, its purposes,
and protection measures.

---

## Data Collection Categories

**Account Information**: Phone numbers and names are collected for account creation and administrator registration purposes.

**Device Identifiers**: We store an identifier for your device to limit access to one device per account, to prevent unauthorized
sharing.

**Enrollment Documentation**: Users submit enrollment proof documents, which remain accessible only to the administrator.

**Learning Metrics**: The app tracks percentage of each video watched, last video viewed, course downloads, and subject
enrollments.

**Notifications**: Push notification tokens enable the service to alert users about courses, enrollment decisions, and live
sessions.

---

## Data Usage & Third Parties

The platform uses data solely for account management, authentication, enrollment verification, notifications, and security. We do
not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

Four external services support operations: Supabase (backend), Agora (streaming), Expo (notifications), and Cloudflare R2 (video
hosting).

---

## User Control & Deletion

Users can access their information within the app, disable notifications through phone settings, and delete accounts via the
Profile page, which permanently deletes all associated data.
