# Privacy Policy — Massar

**Last updated: October 1, 2026**
**Effective date: October 1, 2026**

---

## 1. Introduction

Massar ("we", "our", or "the app") is an educational mobile application that provides video-based learning content to students. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

By using Massar, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Information
- **Phone number**: used for authentication via one-time password (OTP)
- **First and last name**: collected during registration
- **School grade (class)**: selected during registration to personalize content

### 2.2 Enrollment Documentation
- **Enrollment proof documents**: photos or images you submit to verify your enrollment. These files are stored securely and are accessible only to administrators.

### 2.3 Device Information
- **Device identifier**: a unique identifier stored on your device to enforce our one-device-per-account policy and prevent unauthorized account sharing.

### 2.4 Learning Activity
- **Video progress**: percentage of each video watched and last position viewed, to resume playback
- **Downloaded content**: list of videos saved locally on your device
- **Course enrollments**: subjects and chapters you have accessed

### 2.5 Notifications
- **Push notification token**: used to send you alerts about your enrollment status, new content, and live session announcements

### 2.6 Technical Data
- Basic usage data necessary to operate the service (session tokens, request logs). We do not collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking data.

---

## 3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively to:

- Create and manage your account
- Authenticate you securely via OTP
- Verify your enrollment and activate your access to content
- Restrict account use to one device at a time
- Deliver video lessons and track your progress
- Send push notifications relevant to your account
- Enable live session participation
- Allow the AI assistant feature to answer questions about course content
- Delete all your data permanently upon account deletion

We do **not** use your data for advertising, profiling, or any commercial purpose beyond operating the service.

---

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored on Supabase infrastructure hosted in the European Union. Video content is hosted on Cloudflare R2. We apply the following security measures:

- All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
- Authentication uses short-lived JWT tokens
- Enrollment documents are stored in private storage accessible only to administrators
- Device-level restrictions prevent unauthorized access from other devices

---

## 6. Data Retention

| Data type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Account information | Until account deletion |
| Enrollment documents | Until account deletion or rejected and replaced |
| Video progress and downloads | Until account deletion |
| Push notification tokens | Until account deletion or token renewal |
| Session logs | 30 days maximum |

When you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently and irreversibly deleted from our systems.

## 7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate information (name, class) via the app
- **Delete** your account and all associated data at any time via Profile → Delete my account
- **Withdraw consent** by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights or for any privacy-related request, contact us at: **moustaphaissa.pr@gmail.com**

## 8. Children's Privacy

Massar is intended for secondary school students. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 without verified parental consent. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account without consent, please contact us and we will delete the account immediately.
